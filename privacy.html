Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 6, 2026

Welcome to the Holy Scriptures mobile application (the "App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of our App.

1. No Collection of Personal Data

We believe in absolute user privacy. The App is designed as a fully offline utility to search, read, and browse scriptures.

  • We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identification information (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or IP addresses).
  • We do not use any third‑party tracking, advertising SDKs, or analytics software that gathers your personal details.

2. Device Storage & Local Preferences

The App uses your device's local storage (IndexedDB, LocalStorage) exclusively to save reading progress, bookmarks, and UI preferences. This data never leaves the device.

3. Network Connections

The App operates entirely offline and does not connect to the internet except to load bundled resources.

4. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is safe for all ages.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted here with a new effective date.

6. Contact Us

Questions? Visit the GitHub repository: holy-scriptures-app.
